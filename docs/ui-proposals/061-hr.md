# UI proposal 061 — hr (people operations)

Status: PROPOSAL (GUESS where the spec is silent). Sector specialist #061. Read-only on product code.
Grounding: `docs/UI.md`, `spec/08-ui-ux.md`, and
`apps/web/components/{admin/access-screen,thread/thread-composer}.tsx` plus `shell/bot-rail.tsx`.
RPC names CONFIRMED against the exports in `apps/web/lib/api/rpc.ts`; the sector mapping is GUESS
(no "HR" REQ exists in the spec — REQ-058 administration is the closest real anchor).
Charter: Boma Bot only; no upstream/OpenBot brand, code, wording or assets; BoardUI tokens only,
merged with `cx()`, dark mode via tokens; one design system; self-hosted (no SaaS); keep the
robot-cat identity in empty and onboarding states.

## 1. Persona and top jobs-to-be-done

**Persona — Amira, people-operations lead at a 400-person cooperative.** Runs hiring, onboarding,
leave and offboarding, supports French, English and Arabic staff, and works from personnel files
that include national IDs, addresses and salary bands. Boma Bot is self-hosted on the cooperative's
own machine: personnel data never leaves the premises. She is not a technologist; a wrong role
change or a leaked record is a grievance or a privacy breach, so ambiguity and hidden state are
worse than latency.

Top jobs:
1. **Onboard** — create a hire's space access and first-bot workspace, and watch the checklist run.
2. **Authorise** — approve an access grant or role change knowing exactly who is affected and what
   will change, before it takes effect.
3. **Triage** — across a busy week, find paused requests and unread items, snooze non-urgent ones,
   and tell working runs from stalled ones.
4. **Protect** — keep national IDs, addresses and salary bands out of logs, tooltips and other
   members' views; confirm the recipient before a record is shared.
5. **Offboard & audit** — revoke invitations and grants with an attributable reason, and archive
   documents, including when a legacy HR system needs a supervised takeover.

## 2. Five concrete changes

All strings are new keys in `lib/i18n.ts` through `t(locale, key)` (English source; French/Hebrew
translations; a missing key falls back to English, never a blank or the key). All use BoardUI
components under `apps/web/components/` and the semantic tokens in `docs/UI.md`; classes merged with
`cx()` — no raw palette, hex or second design system.

**(a) Record-privacy context strip — screen `/app/:botId` (thread header).**
A compact, read-only band above the transcript: chips for "record open", "retention until <date>"
and "runs working", each with a keyboard-focusable `Tooltip` carrying detail. BoardUI `Chip`,
`Counter`, `Tooltip`, `IconButton` (`components/base/badges/chip.tsx`, `counter.tsx`,
`components/base/tooltip/tooltip.tsx`, `components/base/buttons/icon-button.tsx`); surfaces
`bg-background-secondary-default`, hairline `border-separator-border`, text `text-text-secondary`
/ `text-text-primary`, icons `text-foreground-icon-secondary`, type `text-caption-1-semibold`.
RPCs already present: `botsGet`, `threadsHead`, `runsList`. Bot content is untrusted — render as
text, never HTML; keep personnel identifiers out of the DOM where possible.

**(b) Access-change approval card — screen `/app/:botId` (pending card, REQ-007).**
Make the "what will happen" line dominant: name the person, the role and the exact effect, with an
explicit confirm label ("Grant operator to A. B", not just "Approve"). High-impact changes use
`border-border-error-default` + `text-text-error-primary` **plus** an icon and the words
"High impact" — never colour alone. A failed run offers a named retry. BoardUI `Button`, `Chip`,
`Tooltip`; card surface `bg-background-primary-default`, `rounded-3xl`,
`border-border-button-default`. RPCs: `threadsAnswer`, `threadsReact`, `runsRetry`. The card is
announced once through the polite live region (spec §8.9); the transcript is not a live region.

**(c) Confidential-data composer and reply banner — screen `/app/:botId` (composer, REQ-001).**
Extend `ThreadComposer` with a "contains personal data" `Switch` and a redaction hint; keep
Enter-to-send / Shift+Enter-newline and keep send disabled while an approval is pending. National
IDs, addresses and salary bands stay LTR in `<bdi>`/`dir="auto"` inside the reply chip, never in a
hover-only tooltip. BoardUI `Switch`, `IconButton`, `Tooltip` from the installed base set; focus
`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-border-focus-ring`, 44 px `.boma-target`, icons
`text-foreground-icon-primary`. RPCs: `threadsSend`, `threadsReact` — text enters the existing
`value` state, so no new transport.

**(d) People-and-access triage — screen `/admin/access` (REQ-058).**
In `AccessScreen`, give each member row a named status and a "last seen" column; show pending
invitations with an explicit expiry, and grants with their reason and creator. Make revoke a
confirmed, attributable action whose dialog states what the person loses, and add a named empty
state carrying the robot-cat mark. BoardUI `table`, `Chip`, `Badge`, `Button`, `Tooltip`; surfaces
`bg-background-primary-default`/`bg-background-secondary-default`, hairline
`border-separator-border`, status `text-text-error-primary` plus text+icon. RPCs: `adminMembersList`,
`adminInvitationsList`, `adminInvitationsRevoke`, `adminGrantsList`, `adminGrantsCreate`,
`adminGrantsRevoke`, `spacesInvite`. Emails use `dir="auto"`.

**(e) Document artifacts and supervised takeover — screen `/app/:botId` right panel
(REQ-026/REQ-028/REQ-029).**
A panel lists onboarding policies and signed documents as artifacts newest first, each with a named
status and a "confirm recipients" gate; for the legacy HR system, raise the computer panel with
takeover/release controls. BoardUI `drawer`, `spinner`, `skeleton`, `table`, `Button` (per the
component plan in `docs/UI.md`); surfaces `bg-background-primary-default`/
`bg-background-secondary-default`, hairline `border-separator-border`, status by text + icon.
RPCs: `artifactsListSpace`, `artifactsGet`, `computerStatus`, `computerTakeover`,
`computerRelease`, `computerOpenScreen`. Takeover is explicit and attributable; artifact content
is untrusted — never render its HTML in the document.

## 3. Accessibility, i18n and RTL

- New labels go in `lib/i18n.ts` (English source; French/Hebrew translations; a missing key falls back
  to English, never blank or key). No string in JSX.
- `DirectionProvider` (explicit BCP 47 locale) sets direction; the boot script writes `lang`/`dir`.
  Use logical utilities (`ms`/`me`, `ps`/`pe`, `text-start`/`text-end`, `border-s`/`border-e`) and
  `useDirection()` to mirror arrows and drawer motion in RTL. National IDs, emails, URLs and dates
  stay LTR in `<bdi>`/`dir="auto"`.
- WCAG 2.2 AA gate: zero critical/serious axe findings in light, dark and RTL; 44 px targets via
  `.boma-target`; visible token-driven focus; a full keyboard path (approval, revoke, snooze,
  takeover, recipient gate); status by text + icon, not colour; 360 px width;
  `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured.
- People-data privacy: error copy is one sentence of what happened plus one of what to do — no
  national IDs, addresses, salary bands, stack traces or provider text on screen (correlation id
  behind "details" only, spec §8.9). Nothing confidential lives in a hover-only tooltip; no
  personnel data is written to logs.

## 5. Highest-impact first slice

**Change (b), the access-change approval card.** It is the smallest, highest-value step: one
screen, no new transport (`threadsAnswer`/`runsRetry` already exist), no new component (BoardUI
`Button`/`Chip`/`Tooltip` are installed), and it directly reduces the risk of an ambiguous,
irreversible access decision. Test-first: write `REQ-007 ... names the person, role and effect`
and extend `spec/golden/ui/REQ-007-pending-card.json` first; ship only the dominant consequence
sentence, the explicit confirm label and the text+icon high-impact marker. Change (d) is a close
second because `REQ-058` and its Playwright test already exist. Defer (a), (c), (e) and the new
REQs until the owner assigns ids and approves.
